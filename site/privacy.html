CallScribe

Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

CallScribe is a free, open-source Android app that lists the call recordings your phone already saves, and can transcribe and summarize them using services you choose and pay for. CallScribe has no servers of its own. The developer does not receive, store or see any of your data.

What stays on your phone

  • Your call recordings, their transcripts and AI summaries, your policies and settings are stored only on your phone, unless you turn on one of the optional features below.
  • The API keys you enter for providers are encrypted with the Android Keystore and never leave the phone. They are not included in Android backups or in the Google Drive backup.
  • CallScribe has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.

What is sent, and only when you set it up

  • Transcription: when a call is transcribed, its audio is sent to the transcription endpoint you configured (for example your own RunPod endpoint), or processed on the phone if you chose on-device transcription.
  • AI summary: when a call is summarized, its transcript is sent to the AI provider you chose (Google Gemini, OpenRouter or Anthropic) using your own API key. Their privacy terms apply to that data.
  • Webhook export (optional): recordings are sent to the URL you entered.
  • Google Drive backup (optional, off by default): see below.

Google Drive backup and Google user data

If you turn on Back up to Google Drive, CallScribe asks Google for the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file permission ("See, edit, create and delete only the specific Google Drive files you use with this app"). It cannot see any other file in your Drive.

  • What it stores: in a "CallScribe Backup" folder in your Drive, with a subfolder per phone: call details (time, number or contact name, caller-ID label), transcripts, summaries, policies and non-secret settings, and, only if you turn on Include recordings, the audio files.
  • What it reads: only the files it created in that folder, to back up changes and to restore them to a phone you choose.
  • What it deletes: only backed-up recordings whose cloud retention period, as set in your policies, has passed. They go to your Drive trash.
  • The data goes directly between your phone and Google. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.
  • CallScribe's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can stop at any time: turn backup off (or tap Disconnect Google Drive) in CallScribe → Settings → Google Drive backup, delete the "CallScribe Backup" folder in Drive, and remove CallScribe's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Call recording law

You are responsible for recording and processing calls lawfully where you live. In Israel, recording a call you take part in is legal.
