CallScribe

A free, open-source call transcription manager for Samsung phones. It lists the call recordings your phone already saves, transcribes them in Hebrew with ivrit.ai models, and can ask an AI model for a short summary and a spam / not-spam verdict.

Bring your own services. CallScribe has no servers: audio goes only to the transcription endpoint you deploy (or stays on the phone), transcripts only to the AI provider account you choose, and the optional backup only to your own Google Drive.
